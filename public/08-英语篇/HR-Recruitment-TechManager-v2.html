Building a Tech Team Through Change

主题：人事招聘类 · 适用读者：技术经理

Lila runs the recruitment team at a fast-growing tech startup. Every week, her team opens new positions and reviews dozens of resumes on popular job boards to find the right candidate. Last month, after a major restructuring that followed the company's merge with a smaller competitor, she was asked to lead the entire hiring effort while also handling redundancies from the layoff.

For each new job, every applicant must submit a cover letter and a CV, plus a portfolio if the position involves coding or a copywriter role. Lila's first mentor taught her that a candidate's background often reveals more than their work experience. During the interview stage, she and the hiring manager run a practical assessment to evaluate each candidate's competency and skill set, then invite the strongest ones to an orientation workshop.

This quarter, Lila welcomed two interns as apprentices and offered them a permanent position after their probationary period. Probation, she notes, is a real evaluation. To keep the workload balanced when a senior developer went on maternity leave, she arranged a maternity cover. Meanwhile, the team held a farewell gathering for a long-time employee approaching retirement, and a mandatory training program was rolled out across the department to strengthen every employee's skill set.

Looking at the company directory, Lila sees the team has grown from five to fifteen in just one year. The former entrepreneur who joined last year is now mentoring a new research assistant, and the office intern who once worked as a barista is preparing for her performance evaluation. Lila reminds the team that promotion and a higher salary are earned through demonstrated competency, not seniority, and that every bonus reflects a fair assessment of the employee's contribution.

如今，莉拉的团队已成为公司最稳定的人才引擎。这证明：严谨的合规流程、持续的能力培养和对人的尊重，可以让创意与制度完美并存。
